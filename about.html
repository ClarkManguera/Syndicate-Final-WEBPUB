WHY SYNDICATE
ONE NAME. SHARED PURPOSE.

Great setups are rarely built alone.

Syndicate represents people working together—sharing recommendations, comparing hardware, and helping one another improve.

The name captures a collective of gamers, students, creators, professionals, and technology enthusiasts.

This school-project brand imagines what a transparent, community-oriented gaming retailer could feel like in the Philippines.
